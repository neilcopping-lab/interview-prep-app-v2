Answering with STAR Stories

Predict likely questions from the job spec, then prepare 2–3 versatile stories that cover most scenarios. Tell each one in four parts: Situation (the context, briefly), Task (what you were responsible for), Action (what you did — the actual steps, say "I," not "we"), Result (measurable outcomes, numbers if you can). Record your answer out loud, or just type it — either way, we'll tidy it into a clean STAR structure for your report.

These are a sample of the type of competency questions this role is likely to attract, based on the job description — not a guaranteed or exhaustive list of what you'll actually be asked. Build versatile stories that can flex to cover whatever comes up.
