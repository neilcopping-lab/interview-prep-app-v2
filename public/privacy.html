Our legal basis

  • Building your report, and arranging a coaching call if you book one, is necessary to provide the service you asked for.
  • Recording a voice answer, and hearing from us about future guides and tools, are based on your consent. Both are optional.

How long we keep it

  • Uploaded CV and job description files are deleted from our server as soon as the text has been read out of them.
  • Recorded audio is deleted as soon as it has been transcribed.
  • Your report isn't stored after it's been sent to your browser, apart from what's needed to build your downloadable document in the same request.
  • Your name, email, and the record that you agreed to this notice (plus whether you opted in to hearing from us) are kept as evidence of consent. You can ask us to delete them at any time.
  • Coaching booking details are kept until the session has taken place, plus a short period afterwards for any follow-up.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access, correct or ask us to delete the personal data we hold about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email us at the address above. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Security

Uploaded files are checked for size and type before they're accepted, and deleted as soon as they've been read. Access to hosting and provider accounts is limited to the people who need it to run the service.
